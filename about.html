Our Journey

From One Kettle to a Community

A look back at the milestones that shaped who we are today.

2010

The First Kettle

A single stall. One kettle. Two hands. Our founder started brewing chai on the corner of Brew Street with nothing but a family recipe and unwavering belief.

2014

A Permanent Home

We moved from a cart to a small shop with five tables. The community followed. Regulars became friends, and friends became family.

2019

30+ Brews, One Love

We expanded our menu to over 30 chai varieties and specialty teas. Our signature Kadak Masala became the bestseller it is today.

2022

Chai House Rewards

We launched our loyalty program to give back to the people who made us. Over 12,000 members have joined since.

Today

Still Brewing Strong

We're still here, still brewing, still serving. Every cup is a promise that we'll never compromise on quality or heart.

What We Believe

Our Values

Four principles that guide every decision we make, every cup we serve.

Real Ingredients, Always

No powders. No syrups. No shortcuts. We use whole spices ground fresh, real milk, and premium tea leaves from Assam and Darjeeling. If it's not something we'd serve our own family, it doesn't go in the cup.

Hospitality First

We remember your name. We remember your order. We ask about your day. A chai stall is more than a business — it's a place where people feel seen. That's the standard we hold ourselves to every single day.

Care for the Planet

Compostable kulhads. Paper straws. Local sourcing wherever possible. Bring your own mug and we'll take ₹5 off. Small actions, done consistently, add up to a big difference.

Community Over Everything

We hire locally. We source locally. We invest in the neighborhood that raised us. From sponsoring school events to hosting free chai mornings for seniors, giving back isn't a marketing strategy — it's a responsibility.
