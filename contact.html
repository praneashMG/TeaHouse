FAQs

Questions? We've Got Answers

Quick answers to the things people ask us the most.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We're available on Swiggy, Zomato, and Dunzo for delivery within 5 km. For larger orders (10+ cups), we recommend calling us directly for the best service.

Can I book the space for an event?

Absolutely. We host small gatherings (up to 25 people) for birthdays, book clubs, and corporate meetups. Email us at hello@chaihouse.com with your date and requirements.

Do you have vegan or dairy-free options?

Yes. We offer oat milk and almond milk as substitutes for any chai or tea. Just let us know when you order — no extra charge.

Do you take bulk / catering orders?

Yes, we cater events of all sizes. Minimum order is 20 cups. We offer special rates for offices, schools, and community events. Call us or fill the contact form for a quote.

How does the loyalty program work?

Earn 1 stamp per chai. Collect 8 stamps and your next chai is free. Silver and Gold members earn 2x and 3x stamps respectively. Full details on our Loyalty page.

Are you pet-friendly?

Yes! Well-behaved pets are welcome at our outdoor seating area. We even have a water bowl and a few dog treats on the house.

Do you sell your chai blends online?

Not yet — but we're working on it. For now, you can buy our signature masala blend in small jars at the counter. Ask us about it on your next visit.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes — free parking for 10 cars is available behind the stall. There's also a paid public parking lot 200m away near Central Market.

Still have a question?

We're always happy to chat. Reach out directly.
