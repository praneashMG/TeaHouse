Traditional Brewing

Authentic Handcrafted Blends

Our chai is brewed the traditional way — slow-simmered with fresh spices, whole milk, and premium Assam tea leaves. No shortcuts, no powders. Just real, aromatic chai that warms you from the inside out.

  • No artificial flavors or preservatives

Our Process

From Leaf to Cup

Four simple steps that turn the finest ingredients into your perfect cup of chai.

STEP 01

Source

We carefully handpick premium tea leaves straight from Assam and Darjeeling gardens.

STEP 02

Blend

Fresh aromatic spices are ground and blended in small batches for consistency.

STEP 03

Brew

The mixture is slow-simmered on low heat to extract deep, rich flavors.

STEP 04

Serve

Every serving is poured fresh into your cup, perfectly hot and ready.
